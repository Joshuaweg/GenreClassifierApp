Prototypes

When evaluating the reasoning of the given model we can examine the training data to find the closest neighbors to the inputted text which we call Prototypes.

This gives us the ability to examine how the inputted text is related to features in the training data.

Below is a chart and a table of the closest 20 neighbors to the inputted text.


In the chart you can easily identify the inputted text by the star shape in the plot. This chart is generated by taking 16-dimensional embeddings which describe the relationships between the inputted text and the training data and projecting down to 2D vectors using t-SNE

the other points on the plot represent text from the training data for the model. Note that the points are colored to easily identify how each point is classified by the model
